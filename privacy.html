Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

1. Our Approach

Straya Co Pty Ltd (ABN 89 642 689 076) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle any personal information collected through our website at strayaco.com.au.

2. What We Collect

Our website is primarily informational. We only collect personal information when you voluntarily provide it to us through our contact form. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Any details you include in your message

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry and provide the services you have requested. We do not use your contact details for marketing unless you have expressly consented.

4. Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form handling service. When you submit the form, your data is transmitted to Formspree for delivery to our team. Formspree's privacy practices are available on their website. We do not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or any other tracking technology on this site.

5. Data Storage & Security

We do not store personal information from this website on our own servers. Contact form submissions are held by Formspree in accordance with their data retention policies. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access.

6. Your Rights

Under Australian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Make a complaint about how we have handled your information

To exercise these rights, contact us at vincent@strayaco.com.au.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at strayaco.com.au/privacy.
